# Code of Conduct

DriverUpdater is an open-source community project. We want participation to be welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Our standard

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews, is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive, including when disagreeing.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Focus on the work: the code, the documentation, and the evidence.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish hardware identifiers, logs, or personal data (see [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md)).
- Write in English so that everyone can follow the discussion.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment or personal attacks, insults or demeaning comments based on identity, publishing other people's private information without consent, trolling, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

To report a concern, use the repository's GitHub channels and mention that it is a conduct matter. Do not include private information in a public report. Security vulnerabilities must be reported as described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
